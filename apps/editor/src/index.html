Dotflash

Design temporary tattoos in your browser and print them over Bluetooth.

Arrange your artwork on the sheet, remove backgrounds, and preview every dot before it prints.

This browser can't print. You can still design here, but printing needs Chrome, Edge or Opera on desktop or Android.

Works with

Printer
Hello Blink tattoo maker, listed as WS-171C
Paper
50 × 69 mm transfer sheets

Before you print

  • Close the printer's phone app. The printer only talks to one device at a time.
  • Hold the lid down while it prints, so the right edge comes out dark.
  • Your images never leave this device.
